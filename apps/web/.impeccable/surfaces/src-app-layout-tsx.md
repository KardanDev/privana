## Direction contract

THESIS: Put the creator and their work first, with a recognizable violet shell and familiar subscription-product controls. Avoid a generic metric-card dashboard as the fan experience.

OWN-WORLD: Royal purple actions, pale violet navigation, warm violet-white work areas, humanist Hanken Grotesk UI and Bricolage Grotesque major headings. Rounded content edges; flatter operational rows.

STORY: Fans understand whom they follow and what they can access. Creators manage their current work on a phone. Staff scan real status and data in their own workspace.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop feed has a 232px left rail, a dominant central post and a narrower discovery column with one search. Mobile replaces the rail with bottom navigation. Studio leads with its heading and publication action, then readable counts and content rows; owner views lead with context, totals and a table.

FORM: Proximidade, grounded candidate 1 (independent creator publication/community), chosen over the assigned photographic lookbook and compact catalogue. Its signature interaction is an immediate violet selected-state response with focus retained; opacity/color transitions stay brief and respect reduced motion.
